WHO Guidelines: Menstrual Health, Cycle Variations & Amenorrhea

1. Normal Menstrual Cycle & Common Variations
- Typical Cycle Length: A standard menstrual cycle ranges from 21 to 35 days, with bleeding lasting between 2 and 7 days.
- Normal Variations: Adolescents (ages 12-19) and individuals approaching menopause frequently experience natural cycle variations as hormonal feedback patterns establish or shift.

2. Missed Periods (Secondary Amenorrhea) & Non-Pregnancy Causes
- Overview: Missing a period for 1 to 3 months is a common symptom that warrants calm evaluation rather than alarm.
- Primary Possible Causes:
  • Pregnancy: In sexually active individuals, pregnancy is one common cause among several.
  • Stress & Emotional Strain: Elevated cortisol levels temporarily suspend ovulation by altering hypothalamic signaling.
  • Weight Changes & Exercise: Significant weight loss, low BMI, restrictive dieting, or intense athletic training can pause regular cycles.
  • Hormonal & Endocrine Conditions: Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), thyroid gland dysfunction (hypothyroidism/hyperthyroidism), or elevated prolactin.
  • Birth Control & Medications: Recent start or discontinuation of hormonal contraceptives, IUDs, or prescription medications.
- Recommended Practical Steps:
  • Take a home pregnancy test if sexually active to confirm or rule out pregnancy.
  • Maintain a cycle tracking log noting dates, flow intensity, and associated symptoms.
  • Consult a primary care physician or gynecologist if missed periods persist for 2 to 3 consecutive cycles or are accompanied by unusual symptoms.

3. Dysmenorrhea (Period Pain) & Heavy Menstrual Bleeding
- Primary Dysmenorrhea: Cramping caused by uterine prostaglandin release. Recommended non-pharmacological relief includes warmth/heating pads, adequate hydration, gentle movement, and anti-inflammatory support under provider guidance.
- Heavy Bleeding (Menorrhagia): Soaking through heavy pads or tampons every hour for consecutive hours or passing large blood clots requires clinical assessment for iron deficiency anemia and underlying conditions.

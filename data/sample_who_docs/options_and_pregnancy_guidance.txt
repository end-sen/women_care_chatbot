# WHO Guidelines: Reproductive Options & Clinical Pregnancy Guidance

## 1. Comprehensive Pregnancy Options Guidance
When an individual evaluates their pregnancy, health systems and care providers must offer neutral, non-judgmental, evidence-based information across all reproductive options:
- Continuing the Pregnancy to Term & Parenting: Access to routine antenatal care, social support services, nutritional aid, and community healthcare.
- Adoption & Foster Placement Options: Guidance on legal frameworks, social welfare assistance, and confidential birth planning.
- Medical & Surgical Termination of Pregnancy: Access to safe, legal care performed or supervised by qualified, licensed medical providers in accordance with local clinical standards.

## 2. Medical Overview of Abortion Care (WHO Recommendations)
- Safe abortion is a routine healthcare intervention when performed in accordance with WHO clinical guidelines by trained providers.
- Medical Methods (Medication Abortion): Uses approved clinical medications (mifepristone and misoprostol) administered under clinical protocol.
- Surgical Methods: Vacuum aspiration or dilation and evacuation (D&E) conducted in equipped medical facilities.
- Post-Abortion Care: Immediate access to clinical evaluation, emergency care for complications (such as retained tissue or infection), and post-abortion family planning counseling.

## 3. Important Safety Note on Licensed Providers
- Self-administering unverified substances, non-prescribed dosages, or dangerous home remedies poses extreme risks of maternal hemorrhage, severe infection, uterine perforation, and death.
- All reproductive health decisions and procedures must be carried out in consultation with certified healthcare professionals at accredited health facilities.

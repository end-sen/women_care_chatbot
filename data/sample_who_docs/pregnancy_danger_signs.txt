# WHO Guidelines: Pregnancy Danger Signs & Emergency Triage

## Critical Warning: Emergency Medical Attention Required
Pregnant women, family members, and community healthcare workers must be aware of key danger signs during pregnancy, childbirth, and post-partum. If ANY of the following symptoms occur, immediate emergency clinical evaluation is vital.

## Key Danger Symptoms:
1. Severe Vaginal Bleeding (Heavy Bleeding)
   - Any bright red or dark heavy bleeding from the vagina during pregnancy is a critical emergency (possible placenta previa, placental abruption, or miscarriage).
2. Severe Abdominal Pain
   - Intense, continuous abdominal or pelvic pain, cramping, or rigid abdomen require urgent assessment for ectopic pregnancy, abruption, or uterine rupture.
3. Reduced or Absent Fetal Movement
   - A noticeable decrease or sudden stop in the baby's movements (especially after 24-28 weeks) requires immediate fetal monitoring (cardiotocography/ultrasound).
4. Severe Headache, Blurred Vision, or Dizziness
   - Persistent severe headache, visual disturbances (flashing lights, blurry vision), or swelling of the face and hands are cardinal signs of severe pre-eclampsia or eclampsia (dangerously high blood pressure).
5. High Fever & Chills
   - Temperature above 38°C (100.4°F), shivering, or foul-smelling vaginal discharge indicate serious maternal infection, sepsis, or chorioamnionitis.
6. Premature Fluid Leakage (Water Breaking Early)
   - Sudden gush or continuous trickling of fluid from the vagina before 37 weeks requires urgent evaluation for premature rupture of membranes (PROM).

## Immediate Action Protocol:
- Do not wait for routine clinic appointments.
- Proceed immediately to the nearest health facility with emergency obstetric capabilities.
